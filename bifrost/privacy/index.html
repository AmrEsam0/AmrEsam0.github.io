Bifrost privacy

Bifrost collects no data. It has no servers, no account, no ads and no analytics. What you send goes straight from one of your devices to another over your own network.

App
Bifrost: Local File Sharing
Developer
NullCheck Labs
Effective
18 November 2026

What travels, and where

Files and folders

Only the ones you choose to send, and only to a device on your network that you picked. They go directly between the two devices, encrypted with TLS 1.3, and never pass through a server.

Clipboard text

Sent only when you choose to send your clipboard, or share text or a link to Bifrost. Text that arrives is copied when you tap Copy, or right away if it comes from a device you marked "Always accept".

Your device's name

So other Bifrost devices on the same Wi-Fi can find it, Bifrost announces its device name, system (Android, Windows or macOS) and port on the local network. You can change the name in Settings.

What is kept on your device

Identity and trust

This device's own encryption key and certificate, and the devices you marked "Always accept", saved as their name and certificate fingerprint.

Transfers

The transfer list and a record of which pieces of each file have arrived, so a transfer can pick up where it stopped. Received files are saved to Download/Bifrost, or the folder you choose.

Settings

Your device name, theme, save folder and the addresses you added by IP.

Permissions on Android

Network

Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state and multicast. Used only to find and talk to your other devices on the local network.

Storage

On Android 8 to 10, to save received files to Download/Bifrost. On newer versions, "All files access" is asked for only if you pick a save folder outside Download and Documents.

Nothing else

No location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone permissions. Files you share to Bifrost from other apps are read only to send them.

What it does not do

Bifrost does not collect, share or sell personal data. It has no third-party SDKs, sends nothing to the developer or anyone else, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Backups

On Android, your phone's own backup may include Bifrost's settings and trusted devices, so they come back on a restored phone. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account and its terms, and your phone's backup settings decide what goes in it.

Removing your data

Remove trusted devices in Settings, and clear finished transfers with Clear done. Uninstall Bifrost to remove everything it stored. Files you received stay in your save folder until you delete them.

Children

Bifrost is safe for every age, as it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact
