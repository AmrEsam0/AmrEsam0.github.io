BaseWhiz privacy

BaseWhiz collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and never goes online, so what you type stays on your device.

App

What is kept, and where

History

Conversions and sums that have an answer, saved in a database inside the app when you copy the answer or leave the screen. Turn off Save history in Settings to keep none.

Settings and progress

Your settings, the formats you last picked, your best practice streak and your answer to the question of the day, stored by the app on your device.

Widgets

The home screen widgets show your last conversion and the question of the day, read from the app's own storage. With Save history off, the last conversion widget shows nothing.

On iPhone, the app, its widgets and "Convert in BaseWhiz" share one storage area on your device (an App Group). No other app can open it.

What the app touches

Clipboard

Read only when you choose Paste, or the "Paste and convert" shortcut on Android. Written only when you copy an answer. Never read in the background.

Text from other apps

Text you select and send with "Convert in BaseWhiz" opens in the app's tools on your device. Like anything you convert there, it goes into History unless Save history is off.

Siri and Shortcuts

On iPhone, "Convert number" works the answer out on your device and hands it back to Siri or your shortcut. It is not saved to History.

What it does not do

BaseWhiz does not collect, share or sell personal data. It has no third-party SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and never asks you for a permission. On Android it does not request internet access: the only permissions it declares are a few that Android's own widget library adds for background widget updates, granted without asking.

Links out of the app

Send feedback opens your email app with the address filled in, and you choose what to send. Privacy policy opens this page in your browser. Nothing is sent by BaseWhiz itself.

Backups

BaseWhiz lets your phone's own backup include its history and settings, so they come back on a new or restored phone: Google's backup on Android, iCloud or computer backups on iPhone. Those backups are made and kept by Google or Apple under your account and their terms, and your phone's backup settings decide what goes in them.

Removing your data

Delete entries one by one in History, or clear them all with Clear all in History or Clear history in Settings. Uninstall BaseWhiz to remove everything it stored, settings and streak included.

Children

BaseWhiz is safe for every age, as it collects nothing from anyone.
