BaseWhiz privacy

BaseWhiz collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no network access, so nothing you type ever leaves your device.

App

Developer

Effective
3 November 2026

What is kept, and where

History

The conversions and sums you save, in a database inside the app. Search, pin or delete them in History.

Settings and progress

Your settings, your best practice streak and how the question of the day went, stored by the app on your device.

Widgets

The home screen widgets read your last conversion and the question of the day from the app's own storage on your device. Turning History off in Settings keeps conversions out of the widget.

What the app touches

Clipboard

Read only when you paste or choose "Paste and convert", and written only when you copy. Nothing is read in the background.

Text from other apps

A number you select and send with "Convert in BaseWhiz" is converted on your device and is not kept unless you save it.

Siri and Shortcuts

A number you ask Siri or a shortcut to convert is worked out on your device. BaseWhiz sends it nowhere.

What it does not do

BaseWhiz does not collect, share or sell personal data. It has no third-party SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and asks for no permissions. On Android it does not even request internet access.

Removing your data

Delete entries in History, or uninstall BaseWhiz to remove everything it stored. Backups made by your phone's own system may include the app's data, as for any app.

Children

BaseWhiz is safe for every age, as it collects nothing from anyone.
