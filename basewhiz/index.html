BaseWhiz

A number-base calculator for CS students. Converts between bases, two's complement, IEEE 754 floats and text, and shows every step of the working, the way an exam asks for it.

What it does

Convert

Any base from 2 to 36. Two's complement, one's complement and sign-magnitude at 8 to 64 bits. IEEE 754 half, single and double. BCD, Gray, Excess-3, and text to UTF-8 bytes.

Steps

Shows the working: repeated divisions, place values, and the sign, exponent and mantissa of a float.

Calculate

Exact big-number maths. Bitwise ops in an 8 to 64-bit word with carry, overflow, zero and negative flags.

Bits

A 64-bit bitboard to flip, shift, rotate and invert bits.

Practice

Rounds of ten questions with a streak.

Private

No ads, no account, no internet permission. A searchable, pinnable history stays on the device.
