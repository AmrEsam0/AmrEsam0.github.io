Stash

An offline-first encrypted vault for the secrets developers use every day: env files, API keys, tokens, database URLs, SSH keys, logins and 2FA codes. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads.

What it does

Dev secrets

Env files, API keys, SSH keys, database URLs, logins, notes and 2FA, each with its own form. Folders, #tags and search.

Offline first

A SQLCipher database whose key is wrapped by Argon2id from your passphrase. Biometric unlock through Android Keystore or iOS Keychain.

Optional E2E sync

Syncs phones and Chrome through a hidden appDataFolder in your own Google Drive. Google only stores ciphertext.

Vault health

Flags duplicate, reused and weak passwords. An opt-in breach check asks Have I Been Pwned with only 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash.

Autofill

Android AutofillService and an iOS credential provider extension. Several URLs per login and equivalent domains.

2FA codes

TOTP codes next to their login, from a QR scan, a typed key or a Google Authenticator export. Copied after autofill.

Stash for Chrome

Fills and saves website logins from the synced vault: popup, inline menu in the login field, or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+L. Copies 2FA codes, generates passwords.

Backups

Encrypted .vault files open on Android or iOS with the passphrase. The vault also rides in the phone's own backup, still encrypted.

Import and export

From Google Password Manager, Bitwarden, 1Password, Apple Passwords and KeePassXC. Out to Bitwarden JSON or Google CSV. Credential Exchange (FIDO CXF) on Android and iOS 26+.

Privacy

  • Works offline. Only two opt-in features use the network: sync and the breach check.
  • Sync sends only ciphertext, to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • The breach check sends the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash. Matching happens on the device.
  • Phone backups carry the vault still encrypted with your passphrase.
  • No Stash account and no Stash server.
  • No analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking.
