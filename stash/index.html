Stash

An encrypted vault for the secrets developers use every day: env files, API keys, tokens, database URLs, SSH keys, logins and 2FA codes.

  • Offline first. Everything stays on your device, encrypted with AES-256 and a key derived from your passphrase with Argon2id. No Stash account, no Stash server, no analytics, no ads.
  • Optional end-to-end encrypted sync through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Google only ever stores ciphertext.
  • Stash for Chrome fills and saves website logins from your synced vault.
  • Bring your passwords from Google Password Manager, Bitwarden, 1Password, Apple Passwords or KeePassXC.
  • Privacy policy

Available for Android and iOS, and as an extension for Chrome.
