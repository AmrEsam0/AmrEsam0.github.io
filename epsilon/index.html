Epsilon

Offline-first point of sale for cafés and restaurants in Egypt, with an online ordering menu, an owner phone app and a per-shop customer app.

One order, on every screen. And if the internet drops, the till keeps going.

The product

Windows

Offline POS

Dine-in, takeaway, delivery, split bills, promotions and loyalty. One Windows exe with SQLite that installs and updates itself.

Shop LAN

Kitchen & floor

Kitchen screen or printed tickets, a LAN waiter page with no app to install, a self-order kiosk and a customer display. ESC/POS printing or PDF receipts.

Web

Online menu

Each shop gets a public web menu on its own subdomain. Cash, InstaPay, wallet or bank transfer. Orders land on the cashier with a chime; customers get order-status push alerts.

PWA

Owner app

Live sales and trends, alerts (late kitchen ticket, void, PC offline) and day reports as PDF. Cashflow and staff-pay changes are queued for the POS to apply.

POS

Back office

Stock, suppliers, customers, cashflow, reports, staff pay and attendance. Roles for owner, cashier, waiter and kitchen. Telegram day reports, local and encrypted cloud backups.

Android

Customer app

A branded Android app per shop, built with Kotlin Multiplatform. Arabic and English, Google sign-in.

  • The shop's data lives on the shop PC. The cloud keeps only a bridge: the menu, online orders and their status, owner totals and 90 days of day reports.
  • The POS pushes its changes, pulls new online orders and acks them.
  • Waiter, kitchen, kiosk and display devices are plain browsers on the shop LAN, with 12-hour sessions and SSE for realtime updates.
  • REST and JSON everywhere. No gRPC, no WebSockets.

Engineering highlights

Offline licensing

Ed25519-signed serials bound to the PC, with paid modules (analytics, stock, loyalty, kitchen, kiosk, payroll and more). Verified offline, no license server. Issued from a vendor-side terminal UI.

Signed self-update

The exe polls a release feed, verifies an Ed25519 signature, backs up the database, swaps the binary and keeps the previous one. A rollback button, plus a remote kill switch for a pulled version.

Multi-tenant cloud

One Postgres for every shop. Tenant isolation is enforced by row-level security through a restricted role.

Auth

Customers sign in with Google or Apple per shop; the cloud verifies id tokens, issues EdDSA JWTs and refresh secrets, with per-IP rate limits. Owners link a phone by QR one-time code, then a bearer token.

Sealed backups

Cloud backups are sealed with an X25519 public key. Only an offline private key opens them.

No .env in the field

Shipped config is encrypted at rest with Windows DPAPI.
