What it does

119 stations

22 countries, Egypt first with 24. Pop, tarab, Quran, news, jazz.

Tuner dial

Every station has a channel number. Drag the dial in Now Playing to scrub, release to snap.

Browse & search

Filter by genre or country. Search-as-you-type over name, genre and country.

Background playback

Notification, lock-screen and headset controls. Live track titles when the stream sends them.

Arabic + English

Full RTL. Per-app language on Android 13+.

No account, no ads

Favorites, recents, themes and an offline-cached catalog.

How it's built

LayerTechNotes
SharedKotlin 2.4 Multiplatform, Room KMP:shared module holds models, data layer and ViewModels; Room KMP cache.
AndroidJetpack Compose, Material 3, Navigation Compose, Media3 ExoPlayer, CoilShared-element transitions; ExoPlayer with HLS and MediaSession.
iOSSwiftUI, AVPlayer, SKIEURLSession image loader; SKIE for Kotlin interop.
BackendSupabase Postgres, supabase-kt Postgrest, KtorStation catalog with SQL migrations; OkHttp / Darwin engines.
TestsXCUITestUI test suites on iOS.
