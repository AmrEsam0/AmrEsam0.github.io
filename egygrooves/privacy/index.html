Privacy policy · Android · iOS

EgyGrooves privacy

EgyGrooves has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your favorites and settings stay on your device. To play the radio, the app connects to each station's own servers, which see your IP address, as any website does.

What is kept on your device

Favorites and recents

The stations you favorite, and the stations you tuned to with the time you last played each one. They are kept in a database inside the app and are never sent anywhere.

Station list and logos

A copy of the station list, so the app opens even when you are offline, and station logos cached by the app.

Settings

Your theme choice. The app language is set in your phone's own settings, not stored by EgyGrooves.

What goes over the network

The station list

The app downloads the list of stations (names, genres, countries, logo and stream addresses) from our database, hosted on Supabase. It only reads. It sends nothing about you, and there is no ID or account. Supabase sees your IP address, as any website does.

Radio streams

When you play a station, the app connects straight to that broadcaster's server. These are third parties, not run by us. They see your IP address and which stream you are playing, and they have their own privacy policies. Some stations broadcast over plain http, so that stream is not encrypted.

Station logos

Logos are loaded from the addresses in the station list, which point to the stations' own sites or image hosts. Those hosts see your IP address too.

On your device only

Playback controls

The station, its logo and the song title (when the stream sends one) show in the notification and on the lock screen, and headset buttons control playback. Song titles arrive with the stream itself and are not saved.

Cars

Android Auto shows your favorites, recent stations and all stations on the car's screen, through your phone's connection to the car.

Widgets

The home screen widget shows your last played station and your first favorites. It reads them from the app's own storage on your device.

Sharing and feedback

Sharing a station

Share hands your phone's share sheet a line of text: the station name, its EgyGrooves link and the Google Play link. Nothing is sent until you pick an app, and then it is that app's to send.

Feedback email

The feedback row in About opens your email app with our address filled in. We only see what you choose to send.

What it does not do

EgyGrooves has no account or sign-in, no ads, and no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. It does not collect, share or sell personal data, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Permissions and why

Internet

To load the station list, the logos and the radio streams.

Background playback

On Android, a media playback service with its notification. On iOS, background audio. Both keep the radio playing when you leave the app or lock the screen.

Nothing else

No other permissions are requested on either platform.

Removing your data

Tap the heart again to remove a favorite. To wipe everything, including recent stations, clear the app's storage (Android: Settings, Apps, EgyGrooves, Storage, Clear storage) or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Backups made by your phone's own system may include the app's data, as for any app.

Children

EgyGrooves is for a general audience. It collects no personal information from anyone, children under 13 included.
